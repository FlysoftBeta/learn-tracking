# 应用文专题 01：负责任地使用生成式 AI

## 使用说明

本专题对应“人与社会·科学与技术”和“人与自我·生活与学习”。材料顺序是：先阅读和吸收语言，再做从词组到完整应用文的分级训练。全部做完预计需要 180—240 分钟，明显超过两节课；课堂上可按需要截取。

本专题只在必要处点明技术素养、学术诚信或教育公平，不要求每项任务都上升到宏大价值。训练题为依据高考常见应用文交际功能原创；题库 MCP 尚未接入，因此本版不把任何题目标为真题或题库原题。

## 一、主题事实框架

写作时可从下面四组内容中选取，不必全部使用。

### 1. AI 能做什么

- explain a difficult concept in simpler language：用更简单的语言解释难点
- offer instant feedback on a draft：即时反馈草稿
- provide personalized practice：提供个性化练习
- generate examples for comparison：生成例子供比较
- help organize ideas：帮助梳理思路
- support project-based learning：支持项目式学习
- make learning resources more accessible：让学习资源更易获得

### 2. AI 不能替代什么

- independent thinking：独立思考
- human judgment：人的判断
- original work：原创成果
- meaningful interaction with teachers and classmates：与师生的真实互动
- responsibility for the final answer：对最终答案负责

### 3. 主要风险

- inaccurate or made-up information：不准确或虚构的信息
- over-reliance on technology：过度依赖技术
- privacy and data-security risks：隐私与数据安全风险
- academic dishonesty：学术不诚信
- biased or one-sided output：有偏见或片面的输出
- loss of creativity through copying：因照抄而削弱创造力

### 4. 负责任的使用动作

- define the task before opening the tool：先明确任务，再打开工具
- ask focused questions：提出聚焦的问题
- cross-check key facts：交叉核查关键信息
- compare several possible answers：比较多个可能答案
- revise the output in one's own words：用自己的语言修改输出
- disclose AI assistance when required：按要求说明 AI 的帮助
- avoid entering personal or sensitive information：避免输入个人或敏感信息
- keep the core work student-led：让核心工作仍由学生完成

## 三、应用文功能句

### 1. 提建议

- I suggest using AI mainly for brainstorming, explanation and feedback.
- Before accepting an answer, you had better check it against reliable sources.
- It would be wise to remove personal information before entering a prompt.
- Instead of copying the output, try to rewrite it in your own words.
- Make sure that the final argument reflects your own reasoning.
- If your school requires disclosure, state clearly how the tool was used.

### 2. 说明好处

- Used properly, AI can make difficult ideas easier to understand.
- The tool can provide immediate feedback and practice suited to different needs.
- It may save time on routine work, leaving more room for analysis and creation.
- AI can widen access to high-quality learning resources.
- Well-designed AI activities can encourage students to explore across subjects.

### 3. 提醒风险

- AI may produce convincing answers that are partly or completely wrong.
- Sharing private information may create unnecessary security risks.
- Blind dependence on AI can weaken independent thinking.
- Submitting generated content as one's own work damages academic integrity.
- A fluent answer is not necessarily an accurate or balanced one.

### 4. 发通知与组织活动

- A workshop on responsible AI use will be held in the school library.
- The session will include a live demonstration and a fact-checking challenge.
- Participants are expected to bring one AI-generated answer for discussion.
- Please register through the school platform before Friday.
- Students of all grades are welcome to attend.
- For further information, contact the Science Club.

### 5. 提方案

- I am writing to propose a short AI-literacy week on campus.
- The program could consist of three practical sessions.
- Clear guidance should be provided on privacy and academic honesty.
- Teachers may demonstrate both useful applications and common mistakes.
- A student-made checklist could be shared after the event.
- This plan is practical because it uses existing classrooms and school platforms.

### 6. 表达适度意义

- In this way, students can become more thoughtful and responsible users of technology.
- Such training would help us learn more efficiently without giving up independent thought.
- The aim is not to reject AI, but to use it with judgment.
- What matters most is whether the tool leads to deeper learning.

## 四、可迁移语段

以下语段可拆开学习，不建议整段机械背诵。

### 语段 1：好处

Used wisely, generative AI can serve as a useful learning assistant. It can explain an unfamiliar idea, offer examples and provide quick feedback on a draft. This does not mean that the tool should do the thinking for us. Its real value lies in helping us notice problems and improve our own work.

### 语段 2：核查

AI often presents information in a confident tone, but confidence is not proof of accuracy. When an answer contains an important fact, date or quotation, students should compare it with textbooks, official websites or other reliable sources. Cross-checking takes time, yet it prevents a quick answer from becoming a careless mistake.

### 语段 3：原创与诚信

There is a clear difference between receiving assistance and handing over the whole task. Asking AI to explain grammar or comment on a draft can support learning. Copying a generated response and submitting it as original work, however, removes the learning process and may violate school rules.

### 语段 4：隐私

A useful prompt does not need to include sensitive personal information. Before using an AI tool, remove names, contact details, account information and private stories that are not necessary for the task. Protecting privacy is a practical habit rather than an abstract slogan.

### 语段 5：平衡立场

The question is not simply whether students should use AI. A better question is how they can use it without losing curiosity, judgment and creativity. Schools can set clear boundaries while giving students chances to explore the technology through guided, hands-on tasks.

### 语段 6：活动介绍

To help students use AI more effectively, the Science Club will hold a workshop next Tuesday. Participants will compare several AI-generated answers, identify possible errors and revise one response in their own words. The activity is practical and no previous technical knowledge is required.

### 语段 7：建议信主体

I suggest that the school provide a short guide covering three points: suitable uses of AI, information that should never be shared, and situations in which AI assistance must be disclosed. A clear guide would be more helpful than either a complete ban or unlimited use.

### 语段 8：不强行升华的结尾

I hope these suggestions will make the activity easier to organize and more useful to students. Thank you for considering them.

## 五、基于权威材料改写的阅读材料

这些语篇是训练性改写，不是新闻原文摘录。

### Reading 1: AI literacy at school

China is expanding AI education across different stages of schooling. For senior high students, the focus is moving beyond basic awareness toward practical use, project creation and problem-solving. Schools are also encouraged to combine AI with subjects such as science and information technology. The purpose is not merely to teach students how to operate a tool. They also need to judge its output, understand its limits and use it in a responsible way.

Questions:

1. What is the focus of AI education at senior high level?
2. Why is operating a tool alone not enough?
3. Find two expressions connected with ability development.

### Reading 2: Assistant, not answer machine

In some classrooms, AI provides explanations and immediate feedback. This can help teachers respond to different learning needs and give students more chances to practise. Yet educators warn that the tool should act as an assistant rather than an answer machine. Students still need to ask questions, test ideas and explain how they reached a conclusion. Otherwise, faster homework may result in shallower learning.

Questions:

1. Name two possible benefits of classroom AI.
2. Explain “answer machine” in this context.
3. What contrast is created in the final sentence?

### Reading 3: Rules for generated content

Guidance for schools stresses appropriate and ethical use of generative AI. Students should not present generated material as their own work or use it to avoid genuine thinking. They should also pay attention to privacy, information accuracy and possible over-reliance. These rules do not reject the technology. Instead, they aim to keep learning student-led while allowing useful forms of assistance.

Questions:

1. Which three risks are mentioned?
2. Does the paragraph support a total ban? Why?
3. Paraphrase “keep learning student-led”.

### Reading 4: Equal access

AI tools may give learners in different places access to explanations, practice and digital resources. National platforms can help schools in rural or remote areas share educational content. However, access alone does not guarantee effective learning. Students and teachers also need suitable training, reliable equipment and clear guidance. Educational equality depends on both technology and the ability to use it well.

Questions:

1. How may AI improve access?
2. What else is needed besides access?
3. Is the paragraph mainly optimistic, negative or balanced?

### Reading 5: A practical classroom task

A teacher asks students to use an AI tool to explain why a historical event happened. Instead of accepting the first response, each group checks the answer against the textbook and an official museum website. The students then mark unsupported claims, correct factual errors and rewrite the explanation. In this activity, AI creates material for evaluation, while the students remain responsible for the final result.

Questions:

1. List the four actions performed by students.
2. What role does AI play?
3. Why is this task better than copying the first response?

## 六、人民日报观点转化的情景库

以下情景不是评论摘要，而是把人民日报报道和观点中的真实问题转成高考应用文可用的交际场景。先看清“谁写给谁、为什么写、希望对方做什么”，再选择语言。

### 情景 1：寒假作业一遇难题就问 AI

- 背景：一些家长发现，孩子碰到难题便向 AI 要答案，作文和数学题都由工具迅速代劳。
- 矛盾：即时帮助提高效率，但可能跳过思考过程。
- 写作入口：班主任征求班级 AI 作业规则；你写三条建议。
- 英文题干：Your head teacher is collecting suggestions on the responsible use of AI in holiday homework. Write an email proposing three practical rules.
- 适合规模：60—100词。
- 自然结尾：The aim is not to reject AI, but to make sure that it supports real learning.

### 情景 2：答案很像真的，但事实是编的

- 背景：学生用 AI 查询历史事件，回答中出现一个无法查证的日期和一句虚构引语。
- 矛盾：语言流畅与事实可靠并不是一回事。
- 写作入口：科技社准备开展 fact-checking challenge；你负责写活动通知。
- 英文题干：Write a notice for an AI fact-checking challenge. Participants should bring one doubtful answer and check it with reliable sources.
- 适合规模：40—90词。
- 结尾无需升华：直接写报名方式和携带材料即可。

### 情景 3：AI 帮改作文，边界在哪里

- 背景：同学先独立写作，再让 AI 标出语法问题；另一位同学直接提交 AI 生成的全文。
- 矛盾：反馈属于辅助，整篇代写破坏原创性。
- 写作入口：英语社为校内 AI 使用指南征集示例；你解释两种做法的区别。
- 英文题干：Explain which use should be allowed and which should not, giving one reason for each.
- 适合规模：30—70词。
- 可用核心句：There is a clear difference between receiving feedback and handing over the whole task.

### 情景 4：提示词中泄露个人信息

- 背景：同学准备上传带有姓名、成绩和联系方式的文件，请 AI 分析学习情况。
- 矛盾：个性化建议与个人数据保护。
- 写作入口：给同学发一封简短提醒邮件，并提供安全替代做法。
- 英文题干：Write to your classmate, warning him or her of the privacy risk and suggesting a safer way to obtain feedback.
- 适合规模：30—60词。
- 结尾无需升华：落到 remove, replace, summarize 等具体动作。

### 情景 5：学校是否应该全面禁用 AI

- 背景：发现抄袭后，学校考虑禁止所有学生使用生成式 AI；部分师生主张建立白名单和明确边界。
- 矛盾：全面禁止容易执行，但会失去学习负责任使用的机会。
- 写作入口：向校长写建议信。
- 英文题干：Write a letter suggesting an alternative to a complete ban, including allowed uses, forbidden uses and basic safeguards.
- 适合规模：80—100词。
- 适度观点：Clear guidance may be more effective than either unlimited use or a complete ban.

### 情景 6：AI 进入课堂后，老师做什么

- 背景：学生开始用 AI 获取解题思路、知识图谱和即时反馈，部分传统课堂参与度下降。
- 矛盾：AI 擅长提供信息，教师仍需组织探究、观察学生困难并提供有温度的引导。
- 写作入口：学校举办 “Teachers in the AI Age” 英语论坛；你代表学生提交两个讨论问题。
- 英文题干：Write to the organizer, proposing two questions students would like the speakers to discuss and explaining why.
- 适合规模：60—100词。
- 可自然表达：Technology can provide answers, but teachers help students ask better questions.

### 情景 7：城乡学校共上一节 AI 课

- 背景：国家智慧教育平台让偏远地区接触更多资源，但设备、培训和实际使用能力仍有差异。
- 矛盾：获得资源不等于能够有效使用资源。
- 写作入口：你校与一所乡村学校联合举行线上 AI 学习活动；写邀请邮件或活动方案。
- 英文题干：Invite students from a partner school to an online AI workshop. Introduce the activity, technical preparation and follow-up support.
- 适合规模：80—100词。
- 允许的升华：落到 equal access to learning opportunities，一句即可。

### 情景 8：校园 AI 实验室开放日

- 背景：一些科研院所、高科技企业和教育基地向中小学生开放实验室或展厅。
- 矛盾：活动不能只停留在观看新奇设备，还要形成动手探究。
- 写作入口：写开放日通知，安排演示、项目挑战和交流环节。
- 英文题干：Write a notice for an AI Lab Open Day, including its purpose, two hands-on activities and registration details.
- 适合规模：60—100词。
- 结尾无需宏大：强调 bring a question / work in teams / register by Friday。

### 情景 9：AI 科研“搭子”是否需要标注

- 背景：学生用 AI 整理资料、润色表达或寻找研究线索，却担心标注后显得能力不足。
- 矛盾：合理辅助与透明披露并不冲突；原创性和人工审核仍是底线。
- 写作入口：回复一位准备参加研究性学习展示的同学。
- 英文题干：Write an email advising your friend on how to disclose AI assistance and remain responsible for the final project.
- 适合规模：50—90词。
- 可用核心句：Transparency does not weaken your work; it shows that you understand the limits of the tool.

### 情景 10：未来学习中心参观

- 背景：未来学习空间可能配备智慧屏、可重组讨论区、AI 助教和虚拟实验环境。
- 矛盾：空间和设备先进，不自动等于学习有效；活动设计仍需服务合作与探究。
- 写作入口：给交换生写参观邀请，或参观后给学校写改进建议。
- 英文题干：Invite an exchange student to visit the Future Learning Center and introduce two activities worth trying.
- 适合规模：40—100词。
- 结尾可只表达期待，不必谈国家发展。

### 情景 11：家长担心“有了 AI 还学什么”

- 背景：家长认为 AI 已能回答大量问题，担心传统学习失去意义，也担心孩子不再努力。
- 矛盾：知识获取更快后，提出问题、判断真伪、综合信息和创造表达反而更重要。
- 写作入口：学校英文公众号征集学生观点；写一个回应段。
- 英文题干：Respond to the question “Why should students still learn when AI can answer almost anything?”
- 适合规模：60—90词。
- 观点要落地：用一次学习行为举例，不空喊“AI时代人才”。

### 情景 12：AI 活动究竟有没有效果

- 背景：学校办完 AI 素养周，参加人数很多，但无法判断学生是否真的更会核查信息。
- 矛盾：热闹的活动数据不等于能力提升。
- 写作入口：向组织者提出后续评价办法。
- 英文题干：Suggest two ways to evaluate the AI-literacy week, such as a before-and-after task or a student checklist.
- 适合规模：40—80词。
- 结尾无需升华：说明怎样根据反馈改进下一次活动。

### 人民日报观点的英语化表达

以下为基于中文材料的转述，可作为情景背景，不是逐字翻译：

- AI should reduce repetitive work, not remove the intellectual effort that learning requires.
- Students need to learn where the boundaries of AI use lie.
- Access to digital resources matters, but so do equipment, guidance and the ability to use them.
- Teachers remain essential because education involves judgment, encouragement and human interaction.
- When AI contributes to academic work, originality must be protected and assistance should be disclosed.
- The value of AI in education depends on whether it leads to deeper thought and more meaningful learning.

### D. 句子翻译（12题）

### G. 句式升级（10题）

按括号要求改写。

1. AI is useful. It cannot replace judgment.（用 while）
2. Check the source. Then use the fact.（用 before）
3. Students use AI properly. AI can support learning.（用 if）
4. The answer sounds confident. It may still be wrong.（用 although）
5. We should not copy the output. We should revise it.（用 instead of）
6. The school can set rules. The school can offer training.（用 not only...but also）
7. The activity is practical. It requires no coding experience.（用 because）
8. Students cross-check facts. They become more careful readers.（用 by doing）
9. AI saves time on routine tasks. Students have more time for analysis.（用 so that）
10. Clear guidance is necessary. Responsible use is difficult without it.（用 without）

### H. 信息取舍（8题）

5. 学校考虑全面禁用 AI。提出一个更温和的方案。
6. 同学说“只要英语自然，内容就一定正确”。回应这一观点。
7. 你用 AI 修改英文邮件。说明最终稿仍由你负责的原因。
8. 工作坊结束后，提出一种检查活动是否有效的方法。

### I. 微型写作（12题）

### J. 段落任务（10题）

### L. 快速变式题（20题）

每题限时 2—3 分钟，写 1—2 句。

1. 用 benefit from...while... 写一句平衡表达。
2. 用 not necessarily 写一句准确性提醒。
3. 用 rather than 写一句使用原则。
4. 用 only after 写一句核查要求。
5. 用 what matters is... 写一句结尾。
6. 用 make it easier for sb to do 写一句好处。
7. 用 prevent sb from doing 写一句规则。
8. 用 be expected to 写一句通知要求。
9. 用 consist of 写一句活动安排。
10. 用 be open to 写一句招募信息。
11. 用 raise concerns about 写一句风险。
12. 用 take responsibility for 写一句原创要求。
13. 用 have access to 写一句教育公平表达。
14. 用 in one's own words 写一句修改要求。
15. 用 turn...into... 写一句深度学习表达。
16. 用 provided that 写一句有条件支持 AI 的立场。
17. 用 there is no point in 写一句反对盲目使用的理由。
18. 用 it is advisable to 写一句正式建议。
19. 用 neither...nor... 写一句平衡规则。
20. 用 the aim is not to..., but to... 写一句适度升华。
